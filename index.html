Everything you need

Built for the warehouse floor

No bloated ERP. No expensive subscriptions. Just a fast, focused tool that does exactly what you need.

Interactive 3D Rack View

See your entire warehouse layout at a glance. Boxes grow with stock levels, glow on updates, and flash red when running low — all powered by WebGL.

  • Click-to-select slots
  • Drag-to-orbit, scroll-to-zoom
  • Live animated stock levels

Fast Movement Logging

Stock-in or stock-out with SKU autocomplete, quantity validation, and optional PO/note fields.

Low-Stock Alerts

Items below threshold are highlighted in the 3D view and inventory table, so you never get caught out.

Full Movement History

Every stock-in and stock-out is recorded with timestamp, type, quantity and notes — the last 50 movements at a glance.

Offline-First & Private

All data lives in your browser's localStorage — nothing ever leaves your machine. No cloud, no accounts required to use the app.

Works on Any Device

Responsive layout that adapts from desktop to tablet. The 3D view re-sizes automatically to fit any screen.
